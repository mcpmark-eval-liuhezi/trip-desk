# Tokyo One-Day Plan — Your Labmate's Four Pins

**Base:** Hotel next to Tokyo Station (Marunouchi side). The day starts and ends at Tokyo Station.
**Loop order:** Tokyo Station → **Senso-ji (Asakusa)** → **Shinjuku Gyoen** → **Meiji Jingu** → **Tokyo Tower** → Tokyo Station.

## 2. Why this order

The four pins form a rough rectangle around the city centre. This routing runs **Asakusa (NE) → west side (Gyoen, then Meiji Jingu) → Tokyo Tower (S) → back east**, which is the shortest sensible loop (~22.3 km straight-line, ~27.5 km on foot) and puts each stop at its best time of day:

- **Senso-ji first** — beat the tour-bus crowds that arrive from ~10:30;
- **Shinjuku Gyoen mid-morning** — the big lawns and greenhouse are at their best;
- **Meiji Jingu after lunch** — its forest is deep-shade cool in the early afternoon;
- **Tokyo Tower at dusk** — sunset views, then the tower lights up after dark.

## 3. Stop-to-stop details

### Leg 1 — Tokyo Station → Senso-ji (Asakusa)
- **Mode:** Transit (JR + subway)
- **Route:** JR Yamanote Line, Tokyo → Ueno (~5 min) → transfer to Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Ueno → Asakusa (~5 min) → 5-min walk to Kaminarimon.
- **Time:** ~25 min door to door
- **Distance:** ~4.6 km straight-line; ~5.7 km by street
- **On foot alternative:** ~5.7 km, ~70–75 min (via Ginza/Nihonbashi/Ueno — flat, but dull pavement)

### Leg 2 — Senso-ji → Shinjuku Gyoen
- **Mode:** Transit (subway + JR)
- **Route:** Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Asakusa → Kanda (~7 min) → transfer to JR Chuo Line (rapid), Kanda → Shinjuku (~12 min) → 12-min walk east to the Shinjuku Gate.
- **Time:** ~45 min door to door
- **Distance:** ~8.5 km straight-line; ~10.5 km by street
- **On foot alternative:** ~10.5 km, ~2h15m — this is the leg that kills the walking-only plan

### Leg 3 — Shinjuku Gyoen → Meiji Jingu
- **Mode:** **Walk (recommended — it's short and pleasant)**
- **Route:** Leave by the Okido Gate (east side) and walk east/south through Shinjuku-sanchome toward Omotesando and the Meiji Jingu Harajuku Gate (or continue to the Yoyogi Gate, which is closer from the garden's north-west side).
- **Time:** ~20–25 min
- **Distance:** ~1.4 km straight-line; ~1.8 km by street
- **Transit alternative:** walk 12 min to Shinjuku Station + JR Yamanote Shinjuku → Harajuku (2 min) + 5 min walk = ~25 min (no time saved, worse experience)

### Leg 4 — Meiji Jingu → Tokyo Tower
- **Mode:** Transit (JR + walk)
- **Route:** 5-min walk to Harajuku Station → JR Yamanote Line, Harajuku → Hamamatsucho (~18 min) → 13-min walk south to the tower.
- **Time:** ~38–40 min door to door
- **Distance:** ~4.6 km straight-line; ~5.5 km by street
- **On foot alternative:** ~5.5 km, ~70 min

### Leg 5 — Tokyo Tower → Tokyo Station
- **Mode:** Walk + JR
- **Route:** 15-min walk north to Hamamatsucho Station → JR Yamanote/Keihin-Tohoku Line, Hamamatsucho → Tokyo (~4 min).
- **Time:** ~22–25 min door to door
- **Distance:** ~3.2 km straight-line; ~4.0 km by street
- **On foot alternative:** ~4.0 km, ~50 min

## 4. Foot vs public transit — end-to-end comparison

| | **All on foot** | **Transit (recommended)** |
|---|---|---|
| Total route length | ~27.5 km (5 legs) | ~27.5 km of route, of which you only walk ~4.5 km yourself (Leg 3 + station approaches) |
| Time in motion | **~6h–6h30m** of walking | **~2h30m–3h** including transfer waits |
| Total day length (with ~4.5–5 h of stops) | ~11–11.5 h (08:00 → ~19:30) | ~10 h (08:30 → 18:30) |
| Cost | ¥0 | ~¥900–1,000 total on Suica/Pasmo |
| Practical notes | ~6.5 h on hard pavement in a city with long blocks and constant traffic lights; you'd arrive at Tokyo Tower too tired to enjoy it, and Senso-ji's morning-crowd advantage is lost | Comfortable; luggage-free; you can add detours (e.g., the Sumida riverside or Shibuya Scramble) with the ~3.5–4 h you save |

**Recommendation: transit, with the one scenic walk (Leg 3, Shinjuku Gyoen → Meiji Jingu).**
The four pins are spread over an ~8.5-km-wide rectangle. Walking the whole loop costs ~6.5 hours of pure walking versus ~3 hours by train — that's **3.5–4 hours of your one free day handed back to you**. The Yamanote/Ginza/Chuo lines are frequent (every 2–4 min), cheap, and put you within a short walk of every pin. Tap in with Suica/Pasmo on your phone and you never need to think about tickets.

| Stop | Approx. ground elevation | Terrain note |

**Verdict: essentially a flat day.** The route only gains ~50–70 m of gentle terrace steps across the whole loop, spread over kilometres. The only real ascent is inside the tower. Comfortable shoes are about the terrain, not the hills.

## 6. Lunch

**Maisen Tonkatsu (まい泉), Omotesando** — the route's natural midpoint (between Shinjuku Gyoen and Meiji Jingu, ~12 min walk from the Gyoen's Okido Gate / ~5 min from Meiji Jingu's Harajuku Gate).

- **What:** Hugely popular tonkatsu (breaded pork cutlet) house in a converted 1920s public bathhouse; the kurobuta (black Berkshire pork) loin set is the signature. Consistently ~4.3–4.4/5 on Google Maps across tens of thousands of reviews, and a long-standing Tabelog favourite.
- **Where:** 4-5-6 Jingumae, Shibuya City, Tokyo 150-0001.
- **When:** Opens 11:00; you arrive ~12:45, after the worst of the 12:00 local rush. Budget 60–75 min for a set meal.
- **Order:** Kurobuta loin (rosu) set, or the hire (fillet) if you prefer lean. Comes with shredded cabbage, rice and miso soup — the fuel you'll want before the Meiji Jingu forest walk.
- **Backup:** If you're hungrier earlier, Fuunji (風雲児) in Shinjuku — a ~4.3-rated tsukemen (dipping ramen) shop near the Gyoen — is a solid 10-min alternative before you head east.

## 8. Data sources & caveats

- The Google Maps tools available here (geocoding, place search, elevation, directions, distance matrix) were **unavailable — all calls are blocked by a Google Cloud billing error on the project** ("You must enable Billing on the Google Cloud Project"). No live API data could be retrieved.
- Pin identification is based on coordinate-to-landmark matching against published attraction locations and registered addresses (pins 1 and 2 were additionally corroborated by exact-coordinate records found in a public GitHub dataset: Senso-ji at 2-3-1 Asakusa and Meiji Jingu at 1-1 Yoyogikamizonocho). Pins 3 and 4 match Tokyo Tower (4-2-8 Shibakoen) and Shinjuku Gyoen (11 Naitomachi) respectively, and do **not** match the neighbouring Zojoji Temple (4-7-35 Shibakoen) or Yoyogi Park.
- Distances are calculated from the coordinates (haversine, with a 1.2–1.3 street factor for walking legs); transit times reflect typical Tokyo Metro/JR schedules and transfer walks. **Re-check live times in Google Maps or Japan Transit Planner (jorudan/ekitan) on the day** — expect ±10 min on any leg.
- Elevations are typical published figures for these locations, not API-verified, and are shown as approximations; treat them as "flat city, gentle terrace, one man-made tower" rather than precise survey data.
